Step Recorder is installed

Turn any workflow into a step-by-step guide while you do it.

  1. Pin the extensionClick the puzzle-piece icon in the Edge toolbar, then the pin next to Step Recorder, so it's always one click away.
  2. Start recordingOpen the website where your workflow begins, click the Step Recorder icon, then Start recording. Or press Alt+Shift+R.
  3. Do the workflow as normalEvery click, typed value and dropdown choice becomes a step with a screenshot. A small red toolbar on the page shows the step count and lets you pause or stop.
  4. Stop, tidy up, shareEdit the wording, reorder or delete steps, then download the guide as a single HTML file or save it as a PDF.

Everything stays in your browser. Password and card fields are hidden in step text and blurred in screenshots, but always check a guide before sharing it. You can change the shortcuts at edge://extensions/shortcuts.
